Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Draft template for the Bishkek pilot. Review and adapt with legal counsel before public launch — in particular jurisdiction, the operating entity's legal name, and the data retention periods below.

This Privacy Policy explains how Jaqyn ("Jaqyn", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our loyalty and rewards platform — the customer web app, the business dashboard, and this website (together, the "Service"). The Service operates in Bishkek, Kyrgyz Republic.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: phone number, name, and (for businesses) business name, category, and area.
  • Activity data: visits, scans, reward and campaign progress, vouchers earned and redeemed.
  • Business lead data: details submitted through the "Register your business" form.
  • Technical data: device and log information needed to operate and secure the Service.

2. How we use information

  • To run loyalty programs, campaigns, and group offers, and to verify visits and rewards.
  • To provide businesses with aggregated, privacy-masked activity and reports.
  • To contact pilot applicants and operate, secure, and improve the Service.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. A business sees only privacy-masked customer information (such as a first initial and a partial phone number) for customers who interact with that business. We share data with service providers strictly to operate the Service, and where required by law.

4. Payments

Jaqyn is not a payment app. We do not process or store payment-card data. Payments happen separately through your chosen method (cash, card, MBANK, MegaPay, O!, etc.).

5. Retention & your rights

We keep personal data only as long as needed to provide the Service and meet legal obligations. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us.
