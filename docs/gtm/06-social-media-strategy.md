# Social Media Strategy — Jaqyn

**Who executes this:** 1 technical founder. Time budget: 2–3 hours/week on social
(subset of the 5 hours/week total in `04-marketing-strategy.md`). Every tactic is
Bishkek-specific and executable without a team.

**Grounding docs (do not contradict them):**
- Positioning: `01-positioning.md` — wedge = group "bring friends" + branded card;
  primary message = «Jaqyn возвращает клиентов.»
- Market: `02-market-analysis.md` — real competitors: paper cards (inertia) and
  MBank cashback (free); UDS is the fight only for owners already shopping.
- Marketing: `04-marketing-strategy.md` — Instagram organic + Telegram seeding
  already assigned as channels 2 and 3. This doc is the execution layer for those
  channels, plus an explicit platform verdict on the rest.

**90-day social goal:** Instagram account with visible legitimacy (12+ posts,
200–400 followers including real Bishkek café accounts); 3 Telegram groups with
genuine founder presence; ≥ 5 DM conversations initiated from social; ≥ 2
warm inbound leads traceable to social activity.

## 1. Platform Selection

### The verdict: Instagram (primary) + Telegram channels/groups (primary) + TikTok (secondary, watch-only for now)

The following is an explicit verdict on every platform before selecting. No
hedging — each is either in or out for the 90-day window.

| Platform | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | **IN — primary** | The confirmed storefront for Bishkek ICP-A (cafés) and ICP-B (salons) [verified — `01-positioning.md`]. Owners are active; business accounts are where they post daily. Pre-warm via like/follow before cold DMs is the single most effective trust-building micro-action available. Content (screen recordings, carousels) maps to the format exactly. |
| **Telegram channels/groups** | **IN — primary** | Bishkek café and SMB owners communicate in Telegram groups, not email lists or forums. A genuine founder presence in 3–4 groups converts better than 50 cold DMs [verified — `04-marketing-strategy.md`]. Also the channel for a Jaqyn broadcast channel once 50+ followers exist. |
| **TikTok** | **Secondary — watch-and-seed only** | Strong with young Bishkek consumers (B2C side), not owners (B2B). Consumer-facing content ("watch what happens when you scan a QR at a café") could build end-customer pull for founding cafés. But B2B owner acquisition from TikTok is near-zero. Do not invest in content production for TikTok in months 1–2. Seed one repurposed Reels at month 3 if Instagram Reels are already working. |
| **LinkedIn** | **OUT** | Nearly irrelevant in KG SMB context [stated context]. Bishkek café owners are not on LinkedIn. No ICP discovery happens there. Zero time. |
| **YouTube** | **OUT for 90 days** | Right channel for a 2-minute demo video embedded on jaqyn.kg (host there, embed elsewhere) — but YouTube as an organic growth channel requires consistent long-form production. One person, 5 hours/week, cannot sustain it. Upload demo videos to YouTube as a host; do not build a YouTube channel strategy. |
| **X (Twitter)** | **OUT** | Minimal Kyrgyzstan SMB presence. The few active KG business accounts on X are in English or targeting Russian federal audiences. No ICP discovery. |
| **VK** | **OUT for now** | Declining usage; younger Bishkek audience has migrated to Telegram/Instagram. Not worth time in month 1–2; reassess if Telegram seeding produces VK group leads. |
| **2GIS / local platforms** | **Not social — handled in `04-marketing-strategy.md`** | 2GIS is business-listing, not content. Low-priority listing, not a social channel. |

### Why this two-primary selection is correct

Instagram gives Jaqyn a **visual proof surface** — the most shareable content
(a 15-second screen recording of a group campaign filling with check-ins) maps
perfectly to Instagram Reels. It is also the pre-warm layer before cold outreach
DMs land.

Telegram gives Jaqyn **trust-by-proximity** — Bishkek owners trust peers in
their groups far more than brand accounts. A founder who answers a genuine
question about customer retention in a 200-member café owner group earns more
trust than 10 polished Instagram posts.

These two are complementary: Instagram builds audience and visual legitimacy;
Telegram converts that authority into warm conversations.

### Content pillars — 4 pillars, each with a conversion purpose

| Pillar | Name | What it is | Conversion role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | «Математика лояльности» | Loyalty math — concrete numbers about repeat visit value, retention economics, cost of losing a regular. No product for the first 3–4 slides. | Awareness → consideration. The most shareable content for owners; confirms gut fear with numbers. |
| **2** | «Это работает» (proof) | Screen recordings, dashboard screenshots, wallet card animations, campaign join counts. Shows the product working, ideally with founding café data. | Consideration → decision. The objection-killer: "customers won't use it" dies when you watch 12 people join a group campaign. |
| **3** | «Ваши гости — ваша реклама» | Customer-side perspective: how a guest scans a QR, how a group campaign invite travels via WhatsApp, how the stamp card fills up. | Awareness → trust. Addresses the "customers won't install an app" fear before it's raised. Shareable by founding cafés to their own customers. |
| **4** | «Строим Jaqyn» (building in public) | Founder voice — what was shipped this week, what a customer said, what problem we're solving next. Humanizes the product; builds trust before outreach DMs land. | Trust → warm DM reception. Owners open DMs from founders who feel real, not from brand accounts. |

**Ratio:** aim for 40% Pillar 1 (education), 30% Pillar 2 (proof), 20% Pillar 3
(customer-side), 10% Pillar 4 (building in public) in months 1–2. Shift to more
Pillar 2 once founding café data exists.

### Tone of voice

**The voice is:** a founder who is genuinely solving a local problem, speaks Russian
like a Bishkek business owner (not like a Moscow SaaS marketing team), and treats
the café owner as the expert on their own business.

**Not:** corporate-formal; overly polished; heavy emoji; generic "boost your sales"
language; condescending ("as a small business owner, you should know...").

**Specific rules:**
- Use «вы» (formal you) — this is B2B, not friend-chat
- Short paragraphs; sentences under 20 words where possible
- Numbers always in context ("47 сканирований за 2 недели" not "significant engagement")
- Avoid: «инновационный», «экосистема», «платформа» — these are SaaS jargon, not
  owner language
- OK to use «кофейня», «гость», «постоянник» — owner vocabulary
- One clear point per post. No list-of-7-tips content.
- Never oversell. Founders who acknowledge limitations earn more trust than those who don't.

## 4. Growth Tactics Per Platform

### Instagram

#### Hashtag and keyword strategy

Bishkek-specific hashtags drive organic discovery from local accounts. Group them
into three tiers and use a mix of 6–10 per post (not 30 — Instagram's algorithm
deprioritizes hashtag stuffing after 2022).

**Tier 1 — Hyper-local (always include 3–4 of these):**
```
#бишкек #бишкеккафе #кафебишкек #бишкекресторан #кофебишкек #кофейнябишкек
#бишкекбизнес #малыйбизнесбишкек #кыргызстанбизнес #предпринимательбишкек
```

**Tier 2 — Category (include 2–3):**
```
#программалояльности #лояльностьклиентов #удержаниеклиентов
#штампкарта #кэшбэк #кофейня #barista #coffeekg
```

**Tier 3 — Mechanic-specific (include 1–2, rotate):**
```
#приведидрузей #цифроваялояльность #CRM #ресторанныймаркетинг
```

**Caption keyword strategy (for Instagram search — separate from hashtags):**
Include these phrases in caption text (not just hashtags) since Instagram now
indexes caption text: «программа лояльности Бишкек», «кофейня Бишкек»,
«штамп-карта». One natural mention per caption is enough.

**Keyword note:** Kyrgyz-language versions (#Бишкеккофехана, #БизнесБишкек in
Kyrgyz script) are omitted because the app is RU-only and ICP-A/B are Russian-
primary in their digital presence. Add Kyrgyz hashtags only if and when KY
localization ships.

#### Collab and cross-promo with local communities

**Month 2–3 only** (not month 1 — need content and credibility first):

- **Coffee roasters / coffee equipment suppliers:** Bishkek has several local
  roasters (search «обжарка Бишкек» in Instagram). They serve the same ICP-A
  audience. Proposition: co-post a reel at a founding café — their roast, our
  loyalty card, mutual tag. Zero cash; barter in visibility. Identify 3 accounts,
  follow and engage first.

- **POS resellers (Poster/iiko dealers):** Counterintuitive — a Poster or iiko
  reseller in Bishkek whose clients run cafés is a referral partner, not a
  competitor, for the many cafés that *aren't* on a POS. A DM to the reseller
  account: "many of your clients don't use the POS loyalty module — can we
  complement each other?" One referral from a POS reseller network = high trust.

- **Bishkek food bloggers / café reviewers:** Accounts in the range of 5k–30k
  followers that review cafés. Not for B2B owner acquisition — for B2C consumer
  awareness (customers asking their café about Jaqyn). Budget: barter (free coffee
  at founding café + product access). Target at month 2 when a founding café is
  live and can host the shoot.

- **Business community accounts:** Search «бизнес Бишкек» on Instagram — there
  are aggregator accounts that share entrepreneurship content. A guest carousel
  («5 причин, почему ваши гости уходят») posted by one of these accounts reaches
  ICP-B and ICP-C alongside cafés. Pitch as a content swap: you write a carousel
  for them on loyalty math; they post it with tag.

#### Engagement routine

**Daily (10 min max):**
- Check mentions and DM replies; respond within the hour if possible during
  business hours (09:00–20:00 KGS)
- Like 5–10 posts from Bishkek café accounts followed (keeps Jaqyn in their
  notification feed before outreach DMs)

**Weekly (15 min):**
- Comment meaningfully on 3–5 posts from ICP-A/B accounts: not "nice photo!" but
  something specific ("the seasonal latte concept is smart — does it drive repeat
  visits on its own?"). Comments from a business-adjacent account are read.
- Check if any café you've DM'd posted new content — reference it in follow-up

**Accounts to actively engage (find by searching):**
- Active Bishkek café accounts with 500–10k followers (owner-run, frequent posts)
- Local food bloggers who tag cafés
- Bishkek business community accounts (entrepreneurship content)
- **Do not** spend time engaging with accounts outside Bishkek or with 100k+
  followers (they won't notice your comment)

#### Paid boost strategy (small budget: $15–20/run)

- **Never boost before 8+ organic posts live.** An empty account with boosted
  content reads as spam.
- **What to boost:** the group campaign Reels (Pillar 3 — customer-side demo).
  This is the highest-watch-time content and the best introduction to the mechanic.
  Do not boost carousels — they underperform Reels in the boost algorithm.
- **Targeting (Instagram Ads Manager):**
  - Location: Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan (not KG national — Bishkek only)
  - Age: 25–45
  - Interests: «малый бизнес», «ресторан», «кофе», «предпринимательство»
  - Language: Russian
  - Placement: Instagram Reels only (not Facebook — FB is irrelevant in KG SMB)
- **Budget and timing:** ~1,300–1,750 KGS ($15–20) per 7-day run. Do 1 boost
  per month starting at week 5 (after first founding café is live). KG CPM is
  ~$2–4, so $20 reaches ~5,000–10,000 targeted accounts.
- **Success signal:** DMs received within 48 hours of boost start. If 0 DMs
  from a boost: the CTA in the caption is too soft ("learn more" → change to
  "напишите нам, покажем вживую").

### Telegram

#### Hashtag / keyword strategy (not applicable — Telegram search works on channel names and message text)

**For the Jaqyn channel (@jaqyn_kg):** use short, searchable channel description
with keywords «лояльность», «кафе», «Бишкек», «программа лояльности» — these
appear in Telegram's channel search.

**For group posts:** Telegram groups are closed; hashtags are decorative, not
algorithmic. Focus on quality of reply, not formatting.

#### Target groups (find and join in week 1)

Search these terms inside Telegram:
- «кофейни Бишкек» / «кофе Бишкек» — café owner groups
- «хорека Кыргызстан» / «HoReCa Bishkek» — HoReCa owner/operator chats
- «бизнес Бишкек» / «предприниматели Бишкек» — general SMB entrepreneur groups
- «баристы Бишкек» / «barista KG» — barista community (ICP-adjacent, good for
  word-of-mouth to café owners)
- «ИП Кыргызстан» — sole-proprietor groups; less targeted but broader SMB reach

**Minimum viable:** 3 active groups (> 5 messages/day). If a group is dead,
leave and find the next. Do not invest in low-activity groups.

#### Engagement routine

- **Weeks 1–2:** Observe. Don't post. Learn the vocabulary, recurring problems,
  and active voices.
- **Week 3+:** Answer 1 question per group per week where your answer is genuinely
  useful. Topic areas: customer retention, running a promo, digital tools,
  managing staff. Do not answer off-topic questions.
- **When to mention Jaqyn:** only when the thread is directly about loyalty,
  repeat customers, promotions, or "what tools do you use." Even then, lead with
  the answer, mention Jaqyn at the end: "Мы в Jaqyn решаем именно это — могу
  показать как?"
- **Never:** paste the jaqyn.kg link cold into a group. It reads as spam and
  will get you removed. Earn the right to mention it.

#### Channel posting cadence (once @jaqyn_kg channel has ≥ 30 subscribers)

2–3 posts per week:
1. Repurpose the week's Instagram carousel as a text summary (no image needed —
   Telegram is a reading medium)
2. A short founder observation: 3–4 sentences on something learned from a
   founding café conversation that week
3. Cross-post any significant proof moment (dashboard numbers, case study launch)

### TikTok (secondary — monitor only in months 1–2)

**Do not invest in TikTok content production in months 1–2.** The audience is
B2C (young consumers), not B2B (café owners). The production format (native
vertical video with audio, fast cuts, trending sounds) is a different skill set
from the screen-recording + caption approach that works on Instagram.

**What to watch:** Monitor if any Bishkek food account starts organically
featuring Jaqyn or a founding café's QR. If that happens, it surfaces end-customer
pull — worth a DM to the founding café to repost it.

**Month 3 trigger to reassess TikTok:** If a founding café wants to show their
customers the stamp card on TikTok, help them create the content (they post it,
not the Jaqyn account). Consumer-facing "scan QR, earn stamps" content from an
ICP-A account performs better on TikTok than a brand account anyway.

**Kyrgyz language note for TikTok:** If Jaqyn eventually produces consumer-facing
TikTok content, this is the one surface where Kyrgyz-language audio would have
meaningful reach with young Bishkek consumers. RU/KY bilingual — not RU-only.
Do not produce this until KY localization ships in the app.

## 5. Conversion Path

### The exact path: follower → lead


### Lead magnet ideas (for link-in-bio and Telegram groups)

These are **free downloadable resources** that convert a cold follower into a warm
lead (they give their WhatsApp to get the file).

**Lead magnet 1 — Primary: «Калькулятор лояльности» (Google Sheets)**

A 1-tab spreadsheet where the owner enters:
- Average daily guests
- Estimated % who return monthly
- Average ticket in som
- Proposed loyalty reward (e.g. free coffee every 10 visits)

Output: estimated annual revenue lift from loyalty, cost of the loyalty program,
estimated payback period.

This is the most valuable lead magnet for ICP-A because it makes the ROI personal
and concrete before any pitch. The owner fills in their own numbers; Jaqyn didn't
make anything up.

**Implementation:** create the spreadsheet, host as a public Google Sheets link
(anyone can view, not edit). Caption on the post that offers it: «Посчитайте ROI
программы лояльности для своей кофейни — пришлю таблицу в директ, напишите
'считаю'.»

**Lead magnet 2 — Secondary: «Гайд: программа лояльности за 1 день» (PDF)**

A 4-page PDF (A4, can be designed in Canva in 2 hours):
- Page 1: The 3 loyalty formats that work for cafés (stamps, cashback, group)
- Page 2: The 5 questions to decide which one to start with
- Page 3: What a good table-tent QR card looks like + QR placement tips
- Page 4: The 3 metrics to track in the first month

This is not a Jaqyn sales brochure — it's genuinely useful content. The last
page mentions Jaqyn once: «Если хотите запустить это без бумаги — 30 дней
бесплатно на jaqyn.kg».

**Lead magnet 3 — Conversion: «Шаблон акции "Приведи друзей"» (Canva template)**

A pre-built Canva template for a printed or digital "bring your friends" flyer
that cafés can use in their own Instagram Stories. Pre-filled with example copy;
they change the name and offer. Offered with: «Шаблон акции "приведи друзей" для
вашей кофейни — в директ за 'шаблон'». Converts because it's immediately useful
and visually connected to Jaqyn's mechanic.

**Delivery:** all lead magnets delivered manually via DM for now (no email

## 6. Metrics

### Instagram — track weekly

| Metric | What it measures | 30-day benchmark to double down | 30-day benchmark to change approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DMs initiated** | Whether content is generating outreach intent | ≥ 5 DMs initiated (from posts + CTA) | < 2 DMs — posts are not generating intent; add harder CTAs or change pillar ratio |
| **Profile visits from content** | Whether posts drive profile curiosity (leading indicator for DM) | ≥ 50 profile visits/week from non-followers | < 20 — content is not stopping the scroll; test a stronger hook in the first frame |
| **Saves per post** | Whether B2B-relevant content is being bookmarked (save > like signals professional interest) | Saves ≥ 10% of reaches on education/math posts | Save rate < 3% on all posts — rewrite the content, not the design |
| **Followers who are Bishkek café/salon accounts** | Audience quality (not quantity) | 50+ relevant business followers by day 30 | < 15 relevant followers — posting time, hashtags, or account targeting is off |
| **DM-to-demo conversion** | Whether DM scripts are working | 1 demo booked per 5 DMs sent | 0 demos from 10+ DMs — script is too generic; add specificity about their café |

### Telegram — track weekly

| Metric | What it measures | 30-day benchmark to double down | 30-day benchmark to change approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DM replies received in groups** | Whether group participation is generating conversations | ≥ 1 person DMs you after a group post | 0 DMs after 4 weeks in a group — wrong group or posts are too generic |
| **Jaqyn channel subscribers** | Warm audience who opted in | 30+ subscribers by end of month 1 | < 10 — not enough outreach or the channel value prop is not clear |
| **Mentions of Jaqyn in groups by others** | Word-of-mouth signal | ≥ 1 unprompted mention by end of month 2 | Still zero at month 2 — ask founding customers explicitly to seed the groups |

### Combined — the single most important weekly number

**Demos booked from social sources** (Instagram DM + Telegram inbound):
- Target: ≥ 2 demos/month traceable to social by day 30
- This is the metric that says "the social channels are feeding the outbound
  pipeline." Every other metric is proxy; this one is real.

### 30-day decision framework

**Double-down signals (all good):**
- ≥ 2 demos booked from social
- ≥ 5 Instagram DMs initiated
- ≥ 1 Telegram group producing inbound
- Save rate ≥ 10% on education posts

**Change-approach signals (any one of these):**
- 0 demos from social after 30 days → the social funnel is not reaching ICP-A;
  change either the hashtag/targeting mix or the content pillar (too product-heavy,
  not enough education)
- < 5 relevant Instagram followers → posting time or hashtags are wrong; run the
  calendar for 2 more weeks with #бишкеккафе on every post
- DMs sent but no replies → script is too generic; rewrite Script A to be more
  specific about the recipient's café

## 7. Sustainable Production Workflow

### The 2–3 hour weekly batch

**Principle:** every piece of content is repurposed from something that already
exists — product screenshots, founding café data, or the 10-piece plan in
`04-marketing-strategy.md`. No content is created from scratch.

### Source → output mapping

| Source material | Output pieces | Platform | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| iPhone screen recording of the staff scan flow (30 sec, do once) | Reels: «Вот как сотрудник начисляет баллы — 3 секунды» | Instagram | 15 min edit |
| iPhone screen recording of the group campaign join flow (45 sec) | Reels: «Гость получает ссылку и присоединяется к группе за 1 тап» | Instagram + TikTok (month 3) | 15 min edit |
| Dashboard screenshot from a founding café (2 weeks in) | Carousel: «Что увидел владелец через 2 недели» — screenshot annotated in Canva | Instagram | 20 min |
| The loyalty math from content piece #1 in `04-marketing-strategy.md` | Carousel: 5 slides (100 гостей → 20 постоянников → ROI) | Instagram | 30 min in Canva |
| The honest comparison table from `02-market-analysis.md` (§3) | Carousel: Jaqyn vs UDS vs paper vs MBank (6 slides) | Instagram | 30 min in Canva |
| Founding café owner's quote from the day-21 check-in call | Single post: owner photo (if they consent) + quote | Instagram | 10 min |
| The «3 вопроса» objection script from `04-marketing-strategy.md` Appendix A | Telegram text post + Instagram text-graphic | Both | 15 min |
| Any founding café order or result data | Telegram channel update: «Кофейня [Name] запустила…» | Telegram | 5 min |

### Monday batch session (90 min, replaces the weekly content sprint)

```
09:00 — Pull any new dashboard data from live founding cafés (10 min)
09:10 — Choose this week's post from the 4-week calendar above (5 min)
09:15 — Produce the Instagram post in Canva using the matching source (30 min)
         (Use a template — create 3 Canva templates on day 1: carousel, Reels
          text overlay, single post. Never start from blank.)
09:45 — Write the caption (RU) using the tone rules above (15 min)
10:00 — Schedule the post via Instagram's native scheduler for Tue 12:30 (5 min)
10:05 — Write the Telegram channel version (text-only, 5–8 lines) (10 min)
10:15 — Schedule Telegram post for same day via Telegram's native scheduler (5 min)
10:20 — Review the DM queue; draft any replies (10 min)
```

**Wednesday add-on (30 min):**
- Send 5 outreach DMs (use scripts from Section 5; personalize with one detail)
- Scan 3 Telegram groups; write 1 reply if there's a fit

**Friday check-in (20 min):**
- Log the weekly metrics (DMs, profile visits, demos booked) in a simple 5-row
  spreadsheet
- Note any post that outperformed on saves — replicate its format next week
- If a founding café had a notable week, take a screenshot of their dashboard
  and drop it in a "content bank" folder for next week's proof post

### Content bank (build this in week 1, reuse forever)

Create a phone folder called «Jaqyn контент» with subfolders:
- `Скрины/` — product screenshots, dashboard views, wallet cards
- `Записи экрана/` — QR scan flows, group campaign joins, staff scan
- `Цитаты/` — owner quotes from check-in calls (text notes)
- `Цифры/` — founding café metrics (updated weekly)

When producing content, always pull from the bank first. The only new
creation effort is the caption and the Canva layout.

### Canva template setup (do once in week 1, 45 min)

Create 3 reusable Canva templates at the Jaqyn color palette:
1. **Carousel template** (5-slide set): slide 1 = hook text, slides 2–4 = content,
   slide 5 = CTA + logo
2. **Single post template**: full-bleed warm terracotta background + white text +
   logo bottom-right
3. **Reels text overlay**: black or warm background + large white Russian text for
   muted-playback readability (most Instagram Reels are watched without sound)

Once these templates exist, each post takes 15–20 minutes of content-swapping,
not 45 minutes of design.
