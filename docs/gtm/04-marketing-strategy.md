# 90-Day Marketing Strategy — Jaqyn

**Who executes this:** 1 technical founder. Time budget: ≤ 5 hours/week. Every
tactic is executable without a marketing team, ad agency, or self-serve funnel
(SMS signup is blocked — the first cohort is founder-led and hand-held).

**Grounding docs (do not contradict them):**
- Positioning: `01-positioning.md` — wedge = group "bring friends" + branded card.
- Market: `02-market-analysis.md` — real competitors: MBank cashback (free) and
  paper punch cards (inertia), not UDS.
- Pricing: `03-pricing.md` — 30-day trial; founding rate 690/1,490 KGS; no price
  published yet; manual invoice for cohort one.

**90-day goal:** 15 Bishkek cafés on a live group campaign, 8+ converted to
founding-rate paying customers, founder-facing proof loop documented (scans,
campaign joins, repeat visits) for public case studies.

## 1. Channel Selection

### The channel universe, evaluated

The following is an explicit verdict on every meaningful channel before picking
the three to run.

| Channel | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Founder-led outbound** (DM/call/visit) | **PICK — primary** | 120 pre-scored café leads exist; SMS-blocked acquisition requires personal close; highest-signal for WTP validation; only way to hand-hold onboarding today |
| **Instagram organic + DMs** | **PICK — secondary** | Dominant storefront for ICP-A/B [VERIFIED — `01-positioning.md`]; ICPs spend time there; where you earn trust before calling |
| **Telegram business communities** | **PICK — tertiary** | Bishkek café/HoReCa and small-business Telegram groups are where owners share referrals and advice; zero-cost word-of-mouth seeding |
| SEO / content blog | Defer | Right channel eventually (compounding), but zero indexed authority now and discovery intent in KG is thin for "loyalty software"; ship this at month 3 as a slow-burn seed |
| Paid search (Google/Yandex Ads) | Reject | Search volume for "loyalty program Bishkek" / "программа лояльности кафе Бишкек" is too low to sustain a KG-specific campaign at any meaningful CPL; budget better spent on Instagram boosts targeting local café owners |
| Paid social (Instagram/VK boost) | Conditional | Use micro-targeted awareness boosts (~$20–30/run, targeted at KG café/restaurant owners) ONLY as warm-up before outbound to an owner — never as a standalone acquisition play |
| 2GIS listing / 2GIS Ads | Low priority | 2GIS is customer-discovery for end users; businesses already listed there; no "loyalty software" ad category; worth ensuring jaqyn.kg appears if someone searches a business using Jaqyn, but not a paid acquisition channel |
| Events / barista festivals | Month 2–3 | Right channel (café community, in-person trust); first café festival / coffee weekend in Bishkek is the right venue for live demos; requires at least 2–3 live customer stories first |
| Partnerships (coffee suppliers, POS resellers) | Month 3+ | Productive once you have 5+ cafés running — a coffee supplier or equipment dealer can refer Jaqyn to their customers; zero leverage at zero customers |
| PR / local business media | Month 3+ | KG business press (akipress, 24.kg business section, delo.kg) will run a story once there's a real success case (owner testimonial + numbers) |
| ASO (App Store / Play Store) | Reject | Jaqyn is a PWA, not a native app; no store presence to optimize [VERIFIED — `01-positioning.md` risk #3] |
| Influencer / local food bloggers | Month 2–3 | Secondary tactic: a Bishkek food blogger demoing "earn stamps at [café name]" is social proof for the customer side, not the owner acquisition side; only after first cafés are live |
| Referral program (owner-refers-owner) | Month 2+ | "Refer a business, get a free month" is potent once you have happy founders; build it as a manual process first, no code needed |

### The 3 chosen channels

**Channel 1 — Founder-led outbound to the 120-lead café DB (primary)**
Directly answers why it's right: the business problem is a same-day,
owner-decided B2B sale in a market where trust precedes transaction. A cold
Instagram ad will not close a KG café owner on loyalty software. A founder
who walks in, sits down, opens a laptop, and shows a live group campaign running
in 90 seconds will. The 120-lead DB is pre-scored and ready; the only gating
factor is founder hours.

**Channel 2 — Instagram organic + targeted DMs (secondary)**
Bishkek café and salon owners treat their Instagram as their primary storefront
[VERIFIED — `01-positioning.md` ICP-A/B]. Before any cold outreach call, a
comment or DM from an account that already posts relevant content removes the
"who are you?" friction. Instagram is also where Jaqyn can post its own
social proof (a screen recording of a group campaign in action, an owner quote,
a wallet card animation) — each post warms up the next batch of outreach targets.
Building an organic presence costs only time and compounds.

**Channel 3 — Telegram business community seeding (tertiary)**
Bishkek small-business owners communicate in Telegram groups: café owner groups,
HoReCa supplier chats, barista community chats, entrepreneur networks. A genuine
founder presence in 2–3 relevant groups (not spam — share a useful insight,
answer a question, then mention Jaqyn when directly relevant) costs zero and
generates high-trust inbound. One owner in a group of 300 saying "I'm using
Jaqyn, it's working" is worth 50 cold DMs.

## 2. Per-Channel Playbook

### Channel 1: Founder-Led Outbound

**First campaign: "Café Founder 15" — close 15 founding-rate cafés in 90 days**

Goal: 15 businesses on a live group campaign; 8 converted to founding-rate by
day 90. Founding rate: 690 KGS/mo (Grow) or 1,490 KGS/mo (Business). Manual
invoice; no billing code required.

**The outreach sequence (one café = ~3–5 hours total):**

1. **Day 0 — Pre-warm (~15 min):** Before any call, follow the business on
   Instagram and like 2–3 recent posts. This takes 15 seconds and means the owner
   recognizes the account name before the cold message arrives.

2. **Day 1 — Cold DM or WhatsApp (~10 min):** Short voice message (2GIS typically
   lists a WhatsApp number) or Instagram DM. Script in Russian:
   > «Привет! Я Abdulrahman, основатель Jaqyn — сервиса для кофеен в Бишкеке, который заменяет бумажные штамп-карты и помогает гостям приводить друзей. Хочу показать вам одну вещь — займет 15 минут. Удобно завтра утром?»

3. **Day 2–3 — 15-minute live demo (in-person or video call):**
   - Open jaqyn.kg on phone, show the customer QR scan flow (30 sec).
   - Show the group campaign interface: "come with 2 friends, all get a free
     coffee" — live, on their hypothetical business (60 sec).
   - Show the owner dashboard: scan count, campaign joins (30 sec).
   - Close: "30-day free trial, I'll set it up with you now. Founding rate locks
     in at 690 som/month — the first 25 cafés only."

4. **Day 4 — Onboarding (~45 min with the owner):** Set up the business profile
   together, create one group campaign, print or order one QR table tent, show
   one staff member the scan flow. Leave only when the first scan has been done
   live.

5. **Day 21 — Check-in (~20 min):** Pull up their dashboard together. Show actual
   scans, campaign joins, repeat visits. Convert on proof: "Look — 47 scans, 12
   campaign joins, 3 brought their friends. Want to lock in the founding rate?"

6. **Day 30 — Billing trigger:** Issue the manual invoice (счёт) for month 1.

**Required assets:**

- [ ] Demo account pre-loaded with a café profile, logo, one group campaign, and
      fake-but-realistic scan history (10–15 scans, 2 campaign completions). This
      is the "show, don't tell" engine — build it once, reuse forever.
- [ ] 1-page PDF pitch (A4, RU) with: one-sentence pitch, 3 proof points (café
      frequency, group mechanic, "your own brand"), dashboard screenshot, founding
      price, QR to sign up / schedule. Print 30 copies.
- [ ] QR table-tent template (PDF, printable at any print shop in Bishkek) —
      business-branded, 2 variants (A5 table tent, small sticker). Cost: ~150–300
      KGS to print 10 at a local print shop.
- [ ] WhatsApp/Telegram DM script (RU, 3 variants: cold, warm, referral).

**Weekly time cost:** 4–5 hours/week in months 1–2 (5 outreach targets/week).
Drops to 2–3 hours/week in month 3 as proof builds and referrals warm inbound.

**Monetary cost:** ~0 USD / 0 KGS (founder time only). Print shop for table-tent
physical samples: ~300–600 KGS one-off.

**Leading indicator (4-week check):**
- Week 4: ≥ 5 demos completed, ≥ 3 live businesses on a group campaign.
- If < 3 live businesses: the demo isn't landing. Diagnose: is the objection "my
  staff won't scan" → show the 1-tap scan. Is it "customers won't join" → show
  the group campaign join flow from the customer's phone. Adjust the 15-min demo
  script, not the channel.

### Channel 2: Instagram Organic + DMs

**First campaign: "Behind the Stamp" — 12 posts over 90 days**

Goal: Build a Bishkek café/loyalty Instagram presence with 200–400 followers by
day 90. Not a vanity metric — the goal is that when an outreach DM arrives, the
account has visible legitimacy (profile photo, 12+ posts, local content).

**Content pillars (3 pillars, 4 posts each over 90 days):**

- **Pillar A — "It works" (proof):** Screen recordings of a group campaign in
  action, a wallet card animation, an owner dashboard screenshot with real (or
  demo) numbers. No talking-head videos needed — a 15-second Reels with Russian
  text overlay works.
- **Pillar B — "Here's the math" (education):** Simple carousels — "100 гостей,
  80 приходят один раз / 20 возвращаются. Вот сколько это стоит." ("100 guests,
  80 come once / 20 come back. Here's what it costs.") Loyalty math is the most
  shareable content in this space because it confirms the owner's gut fear.
- **Pillar C — "Your customers are ready" (customer-side):** Short demos from the
  *customer's* perspective — scanning a QR at a café table, watching the stamp
  card fill, sharing a group campaign link in a WhatsApp chat. This handles the
  "customers won't use it" objection before it's raised.

**Targeted DMs (post-follow warm-up):**
After 2–3 weeks of posting (12+ posts live), begin sending targeted DMs to
local Bishkek café accounts. The follow + like already happened (outreach step 1
above). DM when a post of theirs gets good engagement — reference it specifically:
> «Видел ваш новый сезонный лatte — красиво! Занимаюсь лояльностью для кофеен
> в Бишкеке. Покажу кое-что интересное?»

**Paid boost (optional — only with Channel 1 budget leftover):**
One targeted post boost per month — Reels showing the group campaign mechanic,
targeted at KG, aged 25–45, interests: "small business", "café", "restaurant
owner". Budget: $15–20 per boost. Do not boost without 8+ organic posts live
first; an empty account with boosted content reads as spam.

**Required assets:**
- [ ] jaqyn.kg Instagram account (handle: @jaqyn.kg or @jaqyn_app) — professional
      bio in RU: «Возвращаем ваших гостей. Штамп-карты и "приведи друзей"
      кампании для кофеен Бишкека. Пробный период бесплатно →»
- [ ] 3-post content batch (first week): one product demo Reels, one loyalty-math
      carousel, one QR scan explainer. Post all 3 in week 1 to avoid an empty grid.
- [ ] 90-day post calendar (12 posts, dates, pillar assignment, caption in RU).
- [ ] Screen-recording tool (built-in iOS screen record or Loom) — all demos are
      screen recordings, no production cost.

**Weekly time cost:** 1–2 hours/week (1 post per week, 30 min; 5–10 DMs per
week, 30 min; story or Reels engagement, 15 min).

**Monetary cost:**
- Near-zero: $0/mo (no boosts)
- Small: $15–20/mo (1 targeted boost per month)

**Leading indicator (4-week check):**
- Week 4: ≥ 12 followers who are Bishkek café accounts (check manually by
  scrolling followers), ≥ 3 DM conversations started (even "not interested now"
  = a warm signal for month 2).
- If no DM responses: the DM script is too generic. Add a specific reference to
  their last post. Never automate DMs — Instagram's algo flags it and you lose
  the account.

### Channel 3: Telegram Business Community Seeding

**First campaign: "Founder in the room" — 3 groups, 1 contribution/week**

Goal: Authentic presence in 3–4 relevant Bishkek Telegram groups. One mention of
Jaqyn per group per month when genuinely relevant. Target: 2 warm inbound
inquiries from Telegram by day 90.

**Target groups to find and join:**

- Bishkek café / кофейня owners group (search "кофейни Бишкек предприниматели"
  in Telegram)
- Kyrgyzstan HoReCa / restaurant owners group (search "хорека Кыргызстан")
- KG small-business / entrepreneurs group (search "бизнес Бишкек", "ИП
  Кыргызстан")
- Barista community Bishkek (if exists — search "баристы Бишкек")

**Posting rhythm — NOT spam:**
- Weeks 1–2: join groups, observe, do not post. Understand what questions are
  asked, what pain surfaces (staff, suppliers, customers, competition).
- Week 3+: answer 1 question per week per group that is genuinely in your
  expertise (customer retention, digital tools, anything Jaqyn-adjacent).
- When the topic is directly loyalty/retention: "Мы как раз делаем Jaqyn для
  кофеен — вот как мы решили этот вопрос: [brief answer]. Если интересно, могу
  показать."
- Never post a promo link cold. Earn the right to mention the product.

**Referral seeding:**
When a founding café customer is happy (~day 21 check-in), ask explicitly:
> «Вы в каких-нибудь группах для кофейников? Если упомяните нас там, я дам вам
> скидку 10% на следующий месяц.»
One satisfied owner sharing in a group of 200 other owners is the highest-ROI
marketing action available.

**Required assets:** None — founder's personal Telegram account is the channel.
Optionally: a 2-sentence Jaqyn bio for the founder's Telegram profile.

**Weekly time cost:** 30–45 min/week (check groups, write 1 helpful reply).

**Monetary cost:** $0.

**Leading indicator (4-week check):**
- Week 4: joined all 4 target groups, posted ≥ 2 helpful (non-promotional)
  replies, ≥ 1 person DMed you after seeing a post.
- If zero DMs: you are either in the wrong groups (low activity) or posting too
  generically. Pivot to the most active group and answer only the most
  specific questions.

## 3. Content Plan — 10 Pieces

Mapped to funnel stages. Working titles in Russian with English translations.
All are suited for repurposing across Instagram, Telegram, and the website blog.

### Awareness — making the problem feel real

**1. «Почему ваши гости уходят и не возвращаются»**
("Why your guests leave and don't come back")
Format: Instagram carousel, 5 slides. Brief: Loyalty math explainer — 100 guests
walk in, 80 come once, 20 return. Each regular is worth 12× more than a
one-time visitor. No product mention until slide 5. Target: café/salon owners
who haven't thought about retention as a number.

**2. «Бумажная штамп-карта: честный разбор»**
("Paper stamp card: an honest breakdown")
Format: Instagram carousel or Telegram post. Brief: What paper cards get right
(zero friction, universal) and what they get catastrophically wrong (lost, faked,
zero data, no way to know if it's working). Positions the problem — not Jaqyn —
as the subject. Designed to be shared by owners in Telegram groups.

**3. «Акция "приведи друзей" в кафе: как это работает»**
("A 'bring your friends' deal at a café: how it works")
Format: 60-second Reels / screen recording. Brief: Customer-side demo — a guest
gets a WhatsApp message from a friend with a group campaign invite link, taps it,
sees "come to X Café with 2 more friends before Sunday, everyone gets a free
drink." Shows the share, the group check-in, and the reward. No sales pitch —
pure "look what's possible." Most shareable piece in the plan.

### Consideration — helping the owner evaluate

**4. «Посчитаем: стоит ли программа лояльности кофейне с 50 гостями в день»**
("Let's calculate: is a loyalty program worth it for a café with 50 guests/day")
Format: Blog post or detailed carousel. Brief: Walk through a specific scenario —
50 visits/day, 30% converted to loyalty customers, average 2-week return cycle,
+1 extra visit/month from loyalty habit. What does that mean in som? Makes the
ROI concrete for an ICP-A owner. Include a "do it yourself" table they can fill
in with their own numbers. This is the closest thing to a "loyalty math
calculator" the plan can ship without writing code.

**5. «Jaqyn vs бумажные карты vs UDS vs "ничего не делать"»**
("Jaqyn vs paper cards vs UDS vs 'doing nothing'")
Format: Comparison table carousel (Instagram, 6 slides). Brief: Honest
comparison across 4 options on 5 criteria the owner actually cares about: cost,
setup time, analytics, "is it my brand," group acquisition. Jaqyn does not top
every row (be honest: UDS has a bigger existing audience; paper is free). The
structural win is rows 4 and 5 (group acquisition + owner analytics). This
directly handles the "I tried a loyalty app, nobody used it" objection.

**6. «Почему мобильный банк — плохая программа лояльности для вашего кафе»**
("Why mobile banking is a bad loyalty program for your café")
Format: Instagram carousel, 4 slides. Brief: MBank MBONUS gives cashback — but
it builds loyalty to MBank, not to your café. No stamp mechanic ("6th coffee
free" is a stronger habit hook than 3% back), no branded card, no way for the
owner to see who's a regular. Respects the bank product while showing what it
doesn't do. This is the single most important awareness piece — the free
alternative most Bishkek owners are defaulting to right now.

**7. «Как кофейня запустила кампанию "приведи двух друзей" за 20 минут»**
("How a café launched a 'bring two friends' campaign in 20 minutes")
Format: Case study (blog post + shortened Instagram carousel). Brief: Template
for the founding-customer case studies. Walk through setup → first campaign →
dashboard results after 2 weeks. Fill with real numbers from the first live
café. This is the most important content piece in the plan — it is the proof
that converts a warm prospect into a demo request. Write the template now; fill
with real data when the first founding customer has 2 weeks of results.

### Decision — removing the last objection before signing

**8. «Три вопроса, которые задают каждый раз перед запуском лояльности»**
("The three questions every owner asks before launching loyalty")
Format: Instagram carousel or Telegram post. Brief: Directly address the top 3
objections: "my staff won't bother scanning" → show the 1-tap scan + audio
confirm; "customers won't install an app" → show the QR scan-to-join, no
install; "I tried a loyalty app and nobody used it" → show the per-campaign
analytics that tell you within 2 weeks if it's working. Script it as a Q&A with
a real founder voice, not marketing copy.

**9. «Что такое "Основатели Jaqyn" и почему осталось 22 места»**
("What is 'Jaqyn Founders' and why there are 22 spots left")
Format: Instagram post + Story. Brief: Introduce the Founding Customer program —
25 first-paying cafés lock in 690 KGS/mo for 12 months. No pushy CTA, just
clear scarcity logic: "We work with the first cohort hand-to-hand, which is why
we cap it." Designed to be shared by the founder in conversations after a demo
converts.

**10. «Как выглядит штамп-карта вашего кафе в телефоне гостя»**
("What your café's stamp card looks like on a guest's phone")
Format: Reels / screen recording, 30 seconds. Brief: Show the customer wallet
experience — scan QR, card appears with the café's logo and colors, stamp fills
in, progress toward reward visible. No narration needed — just a smooth screen
recording with the café's real branding. The "it's your own brand, not some
aggregator's app" message lands visually, without words. This is the single
highest-converting demo asset; every sales conversation ends with this recording.

All costs in USD and KGS. USD→KGS ≈ 87.

**What you're buying:** pure sweat equity. Every channel operates.

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Outreach (DMs, calls, demos) | 0 KGS (founder time) |
| Instagram organic posting | 0 KGS |
| Telegram community seeding | 0 KGS |

**What this gets you:** 5 outreach contacts/week, 1 Instagram post/week, 3
Telegram groups seeded. Achievable goal: 15 demos → 5–8 live businesses in 90
days.

print materials.

  ave print shops). Branded, A5, stands included.
- $15 contingency / ad-hoc (business card reprints, transport to demos, etc.)
- $50 reserve for month 2 micro-influencer.

### Moderate (~$500/mo — ≈ 43,500 KGS/mo)

**What you're buying:** small stack + 2–3 Instagram boosts + 1 paid
micro-influencer + local event presence + referral incentive budget.

| Item | Cost/mo |

**What this gets you at $500/mo in Bishkek terms:**
- 3 Instagram boosts × $15–20 each: ~15,000 targeted local impressions/mo
- 1 food influencer post (10k–30k followers): reaches an audience that the
  founding cafés' own customers follow, creating end-customer pull ("oh, [café I

- 1 local event table: live demo in front of 50–150 café/restaurant owners.
  One event conversation is worth 20 cold DMs.
- Referral incentive: at $500 budget you can afford 5–6 referral credits/month.

**Recommendation for month 1:** start at near-zero. Validate the demo → close
loop before spending. Move to small ($100/mo) once you have 3+ live businesses
and a working demo script. Reserve moderate for month 3+ when you have a case

## 6. Weekly Marketing Operating Rhythm

**Budget: ≤ 5 hours/week. Non-marketer founder. Every item is concrete.**

This schedule assumes the near-zero or small budget tier. Adjust for moderate
if an event lands on a given week.

### Monday (45 min)

- [ ] **Pull the weekly numbers (15 min):** Check the owner dashboard for any
      live founding cafés — new scans, campaign joins, repeat-visit lift since
      last Monday. Note any business whose scan count dropped (churn signal).
- [ ] **Instagram post (15 min):** Publish the week's pre-drafted post. Use
      Canva or the iPhone photo editing suite — no design tool required. Schedule
      with Instagram's native scheduler (free) for optimal KG posting time: Tue/Thu
      at 12:00–13:00 (lunch scroll) or 19:00–21:00 (evening).
- [ ] **Outreach prep (15 min):** Pull 5 café names from the leads DB for this
      week's outreach. Check their Instagram: are they active? Any recent posts to
      reference in the DM? Note one specific thing about each.

### Wednesday (90 min)

- [ ] **Outreach execution (60 min):** Send 5 cold DMs or WhatsApp messages using
      the pre-written script — personalized with the note from Monday. Log each
      in a simple spreadsheet: name, channel, date sent, status
      (sent/replied/demo-booked/no-reply).
- [ ] **Telegram community check (15 min):** Scan 3–4 Telegram groups for
      relevant questions. Write one helpful reply if a suitable thread exists. Do
      not post if nothing fits — silence is better than irrelevant noise.
- [ ] **Follow-ups (15 min):** Reply to any DMs or WhatsApp messages received
      since Monday. If someone replied but didn't book — send one follow-up with
      a softer ask: "Могу прислать короткое видео вместо встречи?"

### Friday (60 min)

- [ ] **Weekly review (20 min):** Update the leads spreadsheet. What was the
      DM-to-reply rate this week? (Target: ≥ 1 reply per 5 DMs.) Did any demo
      convert to a live business? Log the result against the 15-business goal.
- [ ] **Content draft for next week (20 min):** Write or outline next Monday's
      Instagram post. Pull from the 10-piece content plan above. Batch 2 posts at
      once if time allows — it's faster.
- [ ] **Founding café relationship (20 min):** Call or voice-message one live
      founding café per week (rotate through the cohort). Not a sales call — a
      check-in: "Как идет?" Any friction with the scan flow? Any early signal to
      celebrate? This catches churn signals and produces quotable testimonial
      fragments.

### Monthly (3 hours, first Friday of month)

- [ ] **Review the leading indicators** against the 90-day goals: demos completed,
      live businesses, founding rate conversions. If below target by > 30%, the
      demo script or the outreach target list is the problem — adjust one variable
      at a time.
- [ ] **Update the Founding Customer counter on the website.** If 3 businesses
      paid, the counter should show 22 spots left. Real scarcity compounds over
      time.
- [ ] **Draft one content piece from the 10-piece plan** that is not yet written.
      Commit to publishing it the following week.
- [ ] **Ask one founding customer for a testimonial** (exact words from their
      mouth, not paraphrased). Add it to the landing page.

### What to NOT do weekly

- Do not check Instagram likes/follower count daily — it's noise at this stage.
- Do not run paid boosts without a week of organic data showing the post resonates
  (save/share ratio > like ratio is a good signal for B2B-adjacent content).
- Do not update the landing page copy without a specific reason tied to an
  observed objection.
- Do not spend time on SEO, ASO, PR, or partnership outreach in months 1–2. These
  are month 3+ activities and they compound — but only after the sale motion works.

## 8. What Success Looks Like at Day 90

**Minimum viable outcome (near-zero budget):**
- 15 demos completed
- 8 Bishkek cafés on a live group campaign
- 5 paid at founding rate (3,450–7,450 KGS MRR)
- 1 published case study (real numbers: scans, campaign joins, repeat-visit lift)
- 1 Telegram group where you are a known, trusted voice

**Strong outcome (small budget, $100/mo):**
- 20+ demos, 12 live businesses, 8 founding-rate conversions
- 1 micro-influencer post live
- 2 case studies published on the landing page
- Instagram account with 300+ followers and a 3-post-per-week cadence established
- Referral pipeline: 2–3 cafés referred by founding customers

**At day 90, the strategy shifts:** if 8+ cafés are on founding-rate plans, the
proof loop exists, the case study is public, and the demo script is proven. At
that point: add SEO (a Russian-language blog post targeting "программа лояльности
кофейня Бишкек"), submit to 2GIS as a business tool, begin the
coffee-supplier/equipment-dealer partnership conversations, and open the
referral program formally. The outbound-first phase has served its purpose.
